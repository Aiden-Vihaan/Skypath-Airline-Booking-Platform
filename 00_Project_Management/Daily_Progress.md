# Daily Progress

This document records the development progression of the SkyPath Airline Booking Platform across an eight-week project period during June, July and August 2026.

The timeline is organised around weekly milestones and major design activities rather than documenting every individual working session. Each stage builds upon the outputs of the previous stage.

---

# Week 01 — Project Definition and Product Direction

## Focus

The first week established the foundation of the SkyPath project and defined the problem space from a UX and interaction-design perspective.

The initial direction was to explore the experience of booking an airline journey from the first search interaction through post-booking access.

## Work Completed

- Defined the SkyPath product concept.
- Established the initial project vision.
- Defined the core design challenge.
- Identified the primary booking journey.
- Established initial project objectives.
- Defined the initial scope and boundaries.
- Identified the major stages of the booking experience.
- Established the initial documentation structure.

## Key Outcome

The project moved from a broad airline-booking concept towards a clearly defined UX/HCI design problem.

---

# Week 02 — Market Landscape and Research Direction

## Focus

The second week focused on understanding the broader airline-booking landscape and identifying areas where the experience could be improved.

The emphasis was on understanding how existing booking experiences organise information, present flight options and communicate pricing and fare differences.

## Work Completed

- Examined the airline-booking product landscape.
- Identified recurring interaction patterns.
- Considered common booking pain points.
- Analysed information presented during flight comparison.
- Examined pricing and fare communication.
- Identified potential opportunities for improving clarity.
- Established research-informed design questions.

## Key Outcome

The project direction became more focused on information clarity, comparison, transparency and decision-making rather than simply visualising an airline website.

---

# Week 03 — Competitive Analysis and User Understanding

## Focus

The third week connected market observations with user-centred design.

Competitive observations were used to identify patterns worth reconsidering, while representative personas were developed to explore different traveller priorities.

## Work Completed

- Conducted competitive analysis.
- Identified strengths and weaknesses in comparable booking experiences.
- Developed representative user personas.
- Defined user goals and motivations.
- Identified user pain points.
- Identified user needs.
- Developed design hypotheses.
- Created empathy maps.
- Translated user observations into potential design implications.

## Key Outcome

The project gained a clearer understanding of the different needs that can exist within the same airline-booking journey.

---

# Week 04 — Information Architecture and Journey Structure

## Focus

The fourth week shifted from understanding the problem towards structuring the experience.

The booking journey was decomposed into logical stages so that users could move through the process without unnecessary complexity.

## Work Completed

- Defined the major content areas.
- Developed the information architecture.
- Structured the primary navigation.
- Defined the booking hierarchy.
- Identified relationships between major screens.
- Developed task flows.
- Started mapping user flows.
- Reviewed the sequence of major booking decisions.

## Key Outcome

The project developed a structural foundation that could support subsequent interaction and interface design.

The primary journey was established as:

**Search → Compare → Select Flight → Select Fare → Passenger Information → Seat Selection → Optional Services → Review → Payment → Confirmation → Post-Booking Access**

---

# Week 05 — User Flows and Interaction Planning

## Focus

The fifth week concentrated on interaction-level planning.

The objective was to understand what users need to accomplish at each stage and how one decision affects the next.

## Work Completed

- Expanded the primary user flows.
- Mapped important booking tasks.
- Identified decision points.
- Considered alternative paths.
- Considered selection and confirmation states.
- Planned transitions between major booking stages.
- Identified areas where users could potentially make errors.
- Considered opportunities for progressive disclosure.
- Established the structure required for wireframe development.

## Key Outcome

The booking experience was translated from an information structure into a more detailed interaction model.

---

# Week 06 — Low-Fidelity Wireframes

## Focus

The sixth week moved into structural interface exploration.

Low-fidelity wireframes were developed to test information hierarchy and screen composition before committing to high-fidelity visual design.

## Work Completed

The following core experiences were explored:

- Home and flight search
- Search results
- Flight comparison
- Flight details
- Fare selection
- Passenger information
- Seat selection
- Optional add-ons
- Booking review
- Payment
- Booking confirmation
- Post-booking access

## Design Considerations

The wireframe stage focused primarily on:

- Content hierarchy
- Information grouping
- Interaction sequence
- Primary and secondary actions
- Decision visibility
- Price visibility
- Navigation
- User orientation

## Key Outcome

The project developed a structural representation of the major booking screens without allowing visual styling to obscure underlying interaction problems.

---

# Week 07 — Design System and High-Fidelity Direction

## Focus

The seventh week transitioned from structural exploration towards the visual and interaction language of SkyPath.

The objective was to establish consistency across the interface rather than designing every screen independently.

## Work Completed

- Defined the visual direction.
- Established typography hierarchy.
- Developed colour usage principles.
- Defined spacing relationships.
- Established reusable UI components.
- Considered component states.
- Refined buttons and input patterns.
- Refined cards and selection patterns.
- Considered navigation states.
- Considered feedback and error states.
- Incorporated accessibility considerations.
- Began translating the wireframe structure into a higher-fidelity interface.

## Key Outcome

SkyPath began to develop a recognisable and consistent visual language that could be applied across the complete booking journey.

---

# Week 08 — Final Interface, Documentation and Portfolio Refinement

## Focus

The eighth week focused on bringing the design process together into a coherent case study and repository.

Rather than treating the final interface as an isolated output, the documentation was structured to show how the final experience developed from the earlier research and design decisions.

## Work Completed

- Refined the final interface direction.
- Reviewed the consistency of major screens.
- Reviewed interaction patterns.
- Documented design rationale.
- Documented accessibility considerations.
- Documented design limitations.
- Developed the future roadmap.
- Consolidated project references.
- Organised supporting project documentation.
- Structured the GitHub repository.
- Prepared project-management documentation.
- Reviewed the relationship between the case-study PDF and repository artefacts.

## Key Outcome

The project evolved from a collection of individual design outputs into a structured UX/HCI case study demonstrating the progression from problem definition to final interface.

# Project Progression

The overall development can be understood as a progression through five broad stages:

**Understand → Structure → Explore → Refine → Communicate**

The first stages focused on understanding the problem and users. The middle stages translated those findings into information architecture, flows and wireframes. The later stages focused on visual consistency, accessibility and final interface refinement.

The final documentation then connects these stages so that the project can be evaluated as a complete design process rather than only as a collection of final screens.

---

# Current Status

SkyPath has progressed from an initial product concept into a structured portfolio-level UX/HCI case study.

The remaining work is focused on refinement, consistency review, documentation quality and repository organisation.

Future iterations can build upon the current foundation through usability evaluation, further prototyping and evidence-based refinement.
