# SkyPath — UX Iteration Log

## 1. Purpose

This document maintains a traceable record of design changes resulting from research, usability evaluation, design review, and information-architecture decisions.

The iteration log establishes a connection between:

**Evidence → Problem → Design Decision → Implementation → Validation**

This approach prevents design changes from being documented as purely aesthetic decisions.

---

## 2. Iteration Principles

SkyPath iterations follow five principles:

1. Prioritise user impact over visual preference.
2. Address task-blocking issues before minor refinements.
3. Preserve consistency across related workflows.
4. Validate major changes against the original research objectives.
5. Record the rationale behind significant design decisions.

---

## 3. Iteration Record

### Iteration 01 — Information Architecture

**Trigger:** Initial synthesis of personas, journey mapping, and task flows.

**Problem / Opportunity:**  
The booking experience contains multiple stages that can create cognitive overload if presented without a clear progression.

**Design Response:**  
The booking journey was structured into sequential stages covering flight discovery, comparison, fare selection, passenger details, baggage, seat selection, review, and confirmation.

**Expected Impact:**  
Improve orientation and reduce uncertainty during booking.

**Validation:**  
To be evaluated through task-based usability testing.

---

### Iteration 02 — Baggage Transparency

**Trigger:** Persona and journey-map analysis.

**Problem / Opportunity:**  
Travellers may be uncertain about the distinction between included baggage and paid additional baggage.

**Design Response:**  
The baggage flow explicitly separates:

- Included allowance
- Additional baggage
- Weight limits
- Passenger allocation
- Updated cost

**Expected Impact:**  
Reduce unexpected-cost anxiety and improve decision confidence.

**Validation:**  
Baggage-management usability task.

---

### Iteration 03 — Family Travel Support

**Trigger:** Family traveller persona analysis.

**Problem / Opportunity:**  
Families may require additional information related to children, seating, food, baggage, and travel support.

**Design Response:**  
Family-related needs were incorporated into the research scenarios and usability evaluation framework.

**Expected Impact:**  
Ensure family-specific concerns are represented in the booking experience rather than treated as edge cases.

**Validation:**  
Family travel scenario.

---

### Iteration 04 — Accessibility Considerations

**Trigger:** Accessibility persona development.

**Problem / Opportunity:**  
Users requiring assistance may struggle if accessibility options are hidden within generic booking steps.

**Design Response:**  
Accessibility requirements were incorporated into the information architecture, content hierarchy, and usability tasks.

**Expected Impact:**  
Improve discoverability of assistance and reduce uncertainty.

**Validation:**  
Accessibility-focused usability evaluation.
