# SkyPath — Usability Test Tasks

## Purpose

The following tasks represent realistic airline-booking scenarios derived from the SkyPath personas, journey map, information architecture, and primary user flows.

Tasks are written as scenario prompts rather than interface instructions so that participants are required to discover the appropriate interaction independently.

---

## Task 01 — Search for a Flight

### Scenario

You are planning a trip and need to find a suitable flight between two cities on a specific date.

### Participant Goal

Find available flights that match the required origin, destination, date, and passenger count.

### Success Criteria

The participant reaches the flight-results stage with the correct search parameters.

### Observe

- Understanding of search controls
- Date selection behaviour
- Passenger selection
- Navigation errors
- Unnecessary interaction

---

## Task 02 — Compare Flight Options

### Scenario

Several flights are available. You want to choose an option that provides a reasonable balance between price, duration, and departure time.

### Participant Goal

Compare available flights and identify the most appropriate option.

### Success Criteria

The participant can explain why they selected a particular flight.

### Observe

- Use of filters
- Comparison behaviour
- Fare comprehension
- Price sensitivity
- Information hierarchy

---

## Task 03 — Select a Fare

### Scenario

### Participant Goal

### Success Criteria

### Observe

- Pricing interpretation
- Uncertainty

## Task 04 — Enter Passenger Information
