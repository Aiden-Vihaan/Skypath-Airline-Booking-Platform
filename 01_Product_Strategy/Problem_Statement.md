# Problem Statement

Airline booking platforms often present users with complex interfaces, fragmented booking flows, hidden fees, inconsistent navigation, and limited accessibility.

These issues increase cognitive load, reduce user confidence, and contribute to booking abandonment.

Travellers also face challenges managing trips after booking, including check-in, baggage management, seat selection, and itinerary changes.

SkyPath addresses these challenges by delivering a simplified, transparent, and accessible booking experience that supports users throughout the entire travel journey.
