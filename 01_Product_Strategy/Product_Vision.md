# SkyPath — Product Vision

## 1. Vision Statement

SkyPath aims to create a clearer, more transparent and confidence-oriented airline booking experience that helps travellers make informed decisions without being overwhelmed by the complexity of modern flight booking.

The product vision is centred on treating the booking journey as a continuous user experience rather than a collection of independent transactional screens.

---

## 2. Product Vision

> **To make airline booking easier to understand, easier to compare and easier to complete by designing an experience that keeps users informed and in control throughout the entire journey.**

SkyPath is designed around the principle that users should not have to sacrifice information in order to achieve simplicity.

Instead, complexity should be organised, prioritised and revealed at the appropriate moment.

---

## 3. Vision Principles

### Clarity Over Complexity

SkyPath should communicate complex travel information through clear hierarchy, grouping and progressive disclosure.

The goal is not to remove information, but to make information easier to understand.

---

### Transparency Over Surprise

Important costs, conditions and selections should be visible before users commit to a decision.

Users should be able to understand what they are paying for and why the total changes.

---

### Confidence Over Speed Alone

A fast booking experience is valuable, but speed should not come at the expense of understanding.

SkyPath prioritises confident decisions by giving users sufficient context before important actions.

---

### Guidance Without Restriction

The interface should guide users through complex decisions while preserving their ability to review, change and control their selections.

---

### Continuity Across the Journey

The experience should remain coherent from the initial search through booking completion and post-booking management.

Users should always understand where they are, what they have selected and what they can do next.

---

## 4. Product Experience Vision

The intended SkyPath experience can be described through five characteristics:

### Understand

Users can quickly understand the available flight options and the differences between them.

### Compare

Users can evaluate flights using meaningful criteria such as price, duration, stops and included services.

### Decide

Users receive the information necessary to make informed booking decisions without unnecessary cognitive effort.

### Complete

The booking process provides clear progress, predictable interactions and meaningful feedback.

### Manage

After booking, users can easily access and manage the information associated with their journey.

---

## 5. Target Experience

SkyPath aims to create an experience in which users feel:

**Informed**  
They understand the important details of their journey and purchase.

**Confident**  
They can make decisions without feeling that important information has been hidden.

**In Control**  
They can review and modify relevant choices before committing.

**Oriented**  
They understand their current position within the booking process.

**Supported**  
They receive meaningful feedback when completing important actions.

---

## 6. Product Experience Model

The product vision follows a continuous journey:

**Discover → Compare → Decide → Configure → Review → Complete → Manage**

Each stage has a distinct purpose while remaining connected to the overall experience.

### Discover

Help users search for flights according to their travel requirements.

### Compare

Help users evaluate available options beyond headline price.

### Decide

Provide enough contextual information for users to select an appropriate flight and fare.

### Configure

Allow users to provide passenger information, choose seats and add optional services.

### Review

Give users a consolidated view of their choices and the final cost before payment.

### Complete

Provide clear payment feedback and confirmation.

### Manage

Allow users to access their itinerary and relevant post-booking actions.

---

## 7. Long-Term Product Direction

The initial SkyPath concept focuses on the airline-booking journey.

The broader product direction could extend towards a more comprehensive travel-management experience in which booking, itinerary access, flight status, passenger preferences and post-booking support are connected within one coherent system.

Potential future areas include:

- Personalised traveller preferences
- Intelligent flight recommendations
- Disruption and delay support
- Flexible booking management
- Integrated itinerary management
- Context-aware travel notifications
- Accessibility-focused travel assistance
- More advanced post-booking services

These areas represent future opportunities rather than functionality included in the current design scope.

---

## 8. Success From a User Perspective

The product vision will be considered successful when users can move through the booking journey while maintaining a clear understanding of:

- What options are available
- What they are selecting
- What each selection costs
- What is included in the fare
- What decisions are optional
- What stage of the process they are currently in
- What will happen after completing the booking

The ultimate goal is to reduce uncertainty without reducing user control.

---

## 9. Product Vision Summary

SkyPath is envisioned as a human-centred airline booking experience that transforms a complex transactional process into a clearer sequence of informed decisions.

Rather than pursuing simplicity through the removal of information, SkyPath explores simplicity through better organisation, hierarchy, transparency and interaction design.

The vision is therefore not simply to help users **book a flight**.

It is to help them **understand their choices, make decisions with confidence and remain in control throughout the journey**.
