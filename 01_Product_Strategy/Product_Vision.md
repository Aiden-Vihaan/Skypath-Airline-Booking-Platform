# Product Vision

## Vision Statement

SkyPath aims to redefine the airline booking experience by creating an intuitive, transparent, and accessible digital platform that empowers travellers to plan, book, and manage their journeys with confidence.

Our vision is to eliminate unnecessary complexity from air travel by combining user-centred design, modern technology, and accessibility into a seamless end-to-end experience across mobile, tablet, and desktop platforms.

---

## Vision Principles

- Put users before business complexity.
- Simplify every interaction.
- Ensure transparency in pricing and policies.
- Make accessibility a core feature, not an afterthought.
- Build trust through consistency and reliability.
- Support travellers throughout their journey, not just during booking.

---

## Long-Term Vision

SkyPath strives to become a trusted digital travel companion that assists users before, during, and after every trip through intelligent, personalised, and inclusive experiences.
