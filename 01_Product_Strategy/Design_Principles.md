1. User First – Every feature must solve a real user need.
2. Reduce Cognitive Load – Keep tasks simple and easy to understand.
3. Transparency Builds Trust – Clearly communicate prices, policies, and status.
4. Accessibility by Default – Design for users with diverse abilities from the beginning.
5. Consistency Across Devices – Provide a familiar experience on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
6. Efficiency Without Sacrificing Control – Help users complete tasks quickly while allowing flexibility.
7. Support the Entire Travel Journey – Design for pre-trip, in-trip, and post-trip experiences.
