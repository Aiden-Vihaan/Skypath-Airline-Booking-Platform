# SkyPath — Value Proposition Canvas

## 1. Purpose

The Value Proposition Canvas is used to examine the relationship between the needs of travellers and the value that SkyPath is intended to provide.

The canvas is structured around two perspectives:

**Customer Profile**
- Customer Jobs
- Pains
- Gains

**Value Map**
- Products and Services
- Pain Relievers
- Gain Creators

The objective is to identify where the product experience can directly respond to user needs.

---

# 2. Customer Profile

## 2.1 Customer Jobs

Travellers using an airline-booking platform may need to:

### Functional Jobs

- Search for flights between selected destinations.
- Select suitable travel dates.
- Compare available flight options.
- Evaluate price and travel duration.
- Understand stops and connection requirements.
- Compare fare conditions.
- Select an appropriate fare.
- Provide passenger information.
- Select seats.
- Add optional services.
- Review the complete booking.
- Complete payment.
- Access booking confirmation.
- Retrieve post-booking information.

### Decision-Making Jobs

Travellers also need to:

- Determine which flight provides the best overall value.
- Understand the trade-offs between cheaper and more convenient options.
- Decide which fare is appropriate for their needs.
- Determine whether optional services are worthwhile.
- Confirm that the final booking matches their expectations.

### Emotional Jobs

Travellers want to:

- Feel confident about their purchase.
- Avoid making expensive mistakes.
- Feel in control of their choices.
- Avoid uncertainty during payment.
- Feel reassured after completing the booking.

---

# 3. Customer Pains

## 3.1 Information-Related Pains

Users may encounter difficulty when:

- Too much information is presented simultaneously.
- Important information is visually buried.
- Flight options are difficult to compare.
- Fare differences are unclear.
- Baggage conditions are difficult to identify.
- Important restrictions are not immediately visible.

---

## 3.2 Financial Pains

Users may experience uncertainty when:

- The displayed price changes during the journey.
- Additional services increase the total unexpectedly.
- Optional charges are difficult to distinguish from mandatory charges.
- The final amount is not immediately understandable.

---

## 3.3 Interaction Pains

Users may experience friction when:

- Forms contain too many fields.
- Information has to be entered repeatedly.
- Navigation between booking stages is unclear.
- Previous selections are difficult to review.
- Correcting mistakes requires unnecessary repetition.

---

## 3.4 Decision Pains

Users may struggle when:

- Multiple flights appear similar.
- Price is presented without enough context.
- Fare packages contain complex differences.
- Optional services are introduced without sufficient explanation.
- The consequences of a selection are unclear.

---

## 3.5 Trust and Confidence Pains

Users may become uncertain when:

- They do not know whether an action succeeded.
- Payment status is unclear.
- Their selections are not visibly confirmed.
- Booking confirmation is difficult to locate.
- They are unsure what happens after payment.

---

# 4. Customer Gains

## 4.1 Functional Gains

Users want to:

- Find suitable flights efficiently.
- Compare relevant options easily.
- Understand fare differences.
- Complete booking with minimal unnecessary effort.
- Review their complete booking before payment.
- Access their itinerary afterwards.

---

## 4.2 Cognitive Gains

Users want:

- Clear information hierarchy.
- Reduced cognitive load.
- Easier comparison.
- Better understanding of pricing.
- Clear decision points.
- Predictable interactions.

---

## 4.3 Emotional Gains

Users want to feel:

- Confident
- Informed
- Reassured
- In control
- Comfortable with their purchase

---

## 4.4 Long-Term Gains

After booking, users benefit from:

- Easy access to their itinerary.
- Clear post-booking information.
- Reduced uncertainty about their journey.
- Continued access to relevant travel information.
- A consistent relationship with the product.

# 6. Pain Relievers

## 6.1 Reducing Information Overload

SkyPath uses hierarchy and structured information grouping to prioritise the information required for the current decision.

Secondary information can be presented without competing with the primary decision.

---

## 6.2 Improving Flight Comparison

Flight results are structured around meaningful comparison criteria such as:

- Price
- Departure time
- Arrival time
- Duration
- Stops
- Relevant fare information

This allows users to compare options without relying on price alone.

---

## 6.3 Improving Price Transparency

Price information is maintained at relevant decision points throughout the journey.

The final review provides a consolidated view of the booking cost before payment.

---

## 6.4 Reducing Unnecessary Interaction Effort

The booking flow is structured so that users progress through related decisions in a logical sequence.

The experience avoids unnecessary repetition where possible.

---

## 6.5 Reducing Decision Uncertainty

The interface provides contextual information at important decision points.

Users can understand the implications of a selection before moving forward.

---

## 6.6 Supporting Error Recovery

The experience considers validation, feedback and review stages so that users have opportunities to identify and correct problems before final commitment.

---

# 7. Gain Creators

## 7.1 Confidence Through Transparency

Users gain confidence when they can understand:

- What they are selecting.
- What is included.
- What is optional.
- What the selection costs.
- What the final amount will be.

---

## 7.2 Confidence Through Consistency

Consistent interaction patterns reduce the amount users need to learn during the journey.

Buttons, forms, cards, selections and feedback should behave predictably.

---

## 7.3 Better Decision Quality

Structured comparison and contextual information help users make decisions based on their actual priorities rather than relying only on the most visually prominent option.

---

## 7.4 Reduced Cognitive Effort

The experience aims to reduce unnecessary mental processing by:

- Grouping related information.
- Establishing clear hierarchy.
- Separating essential and optional decisions.
- Maintaining relevant context.
- Providing information when it is needed.

---

## 7.5 Continuity After Purchase

Post-booking access extends the value of the product beyond the transaction.

Users can continue to access relevant journey information after completing the initial booking.

---

# 8. Value Proposition

The central SkyPath value proposition is:

> **SkyPath helps travellers make better-informed flight decisions and complete bookings with greater confidence by combining transparent pricing, meaningful comparison and a structured end-to-end booking experience.**

The proposition is built around three core values:

### Understand

Make complex airline information easier to interpret.

### Decide

Help users compare options and understand trade-offs.

### Complete

Provide a predictable and transparent path from selection to confirmation.

---

# 9. Value Proposition Fit

The strongest product opportunities occur where significant customer pains align directly with product responses.

| Customer Pain | SkyPath Response | Expected Value |
|---|---|---|
| Difficult flight comparison | Structured comparison | Easier decision-making |
| Unclear fare differences | Contextual fare information | Better understanding |
| Unexpected additional costs | Transparent pricing | Greater trust |
| Excessive information | Hierarchical presentation | Lower cognitive load |
| Unclear progress | Journey and state visibility | Better orientation |
| Repetitive interaction | Structured forms and flow | Lower effort |
| Payment uncertainty | Clear system feedback | Greater reassurance |
| Difficult post-booking access | Connected booking management | Better continuity |

---

# 10. Value Proposition Priorities

The value proposition is prioritised around the following hierarchy:

**1. Clarity**

Users should understand what they are seeing.

**2. Transparency**

Users should understand what they are paying for.

**3. Comparison**

Users should understand how options differ.

**4. Control**

Users should be able to review and change appropriate selections.

**5. Continuity**

Users should remain connected to their booking after payment.

---

# 11. Strategic Value

The value of SkyPath is not based on reducing the airline-booking journey to the fewest possible screens.

Instead, the product explores whether a complex journey can feel simpler through better information architecture, interaction design and visual hierarchy.

The central proposition is therefore:

> **Complexity does not always need to be removed. It needs to be organised.**

SkyPath uses this principle to balance information richness with usability.

---

# 12. Assumptions

The value proposition currently represents a design hypothesis rather than a validated commercial proposition.

The project assumes that:

- Users value transparent pricing.
- Users benefit from structured flight comparison.
- Users experience unnecessary cognitive effort during complex booking journeys.
- Clearer information hierarchy can improve decision confidence.
- Consistent interaction patterns can reduce unnecessary friction.
- Better post-booking continuity can improve the overall travel experience.

These assumptions would require validation through appropriate user research and usability evaluation.

---

# 13. Validation Direction

Future validation could examine whether the proposed value is actually experienced by users.

Potential evaluation questions include:

- Can users identify a suitable flight efficiently?
- Can users explain why they selected one flight over another?
- Can users understand fare differences?
- Can users identify the final price before payment?
- Can users distinguish optional services from required booking information?
- Can users recover from common errors?
- Can users locate their booking after confirmation?
- Do users report greater confidence during the booking process?

These questions can guide future usability testing and iterative refinement.

---

# 14. Summary

The SkyPath Value Proposition Canvas connects traveller needs with the proposed product experience.

The central relationship is:

**User Pains → Design Responses → Improved Understanding → Greater Confidence**

SkyPath therefore positions itself around a simple product idea:

> **Help travellers make complex booking decisions feel clear, transparent and manageable without taking control away from them.**
