# Value Proposition

## Customer Jobs

Travellers need to:

- Find suitable flights.
- Compare prices.
- Book tickets securely.
- Manage trips.
- Receive travel updates.

---

## Customer Pains

- Hidden fees
- Complicated booking process
- Poor mobile usability
- Confusing navigation
- Limited accessibility
- Booking anxiety

---

## Customer Gains

- Faster booking
- Transparent pricing
- Easy trip management
- Accessible interface
- Personalised recommendations
- Greater confidence throughout the journey

---

## SkyPath Value

SkyPath delivers a user-centred airline booking experience that combines simplicity, accessibility, transparency, and intelligent travel assistance into one seamless platform.
