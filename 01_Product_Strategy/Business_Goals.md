# SkyPath — Business Goals

## 1. Purpose

The business goals define the product outcomes that SkyPath is intended to support alongside its user-centred objectives.

Although SkyPath is primarily explored as a UX and HCI project, a viable digital product must balance user needs with operational, commercial and long-term product considerations.

The business perspective therefore focuses on creating a booking experience that improves decision quality, reduces avoidable friction and establishes a foundation for continued customer interaction.

---

## 2. Primary Business Goal

> **Create a clear and trustworthy airline-booking experience that supports successful booking completion while reducing avoidable friction throughout the customer journey.**

The primary objective is not simply to maximise the number of completed transactions.

A stronger product experience should help users make appropriate decisions while reducing confusion, abandonment and unnecessary support requirements.

---

## 3. Business Objective 01 — Improve Booking Conversion

SkyPath should reduce unnecessary friction between flight discovery and booking completion.

A clearer journey can help users progress through:

**Search → Compare → Select → Configure → Review → Payment → Confirmation**

### Desired Business Outcome

A greater proportion of users who begin a booking journey should be able to complete it without encountering unnecessary interaction barriers.

---

## 4. Business Objective 02 — Reduce Booking Abandonment

Complex booking journeys can create points where users hesitate or leave the process.

Potential friction points include:

- Difficult flight comparison
- Unclear pricing
- Unexpected additional costs
- Excessive form completion
- Confusing fare conditions
- Unclear progress
- Uncertainty during payment

SkyPath aims to identify and reduce these friction points through clearer interaction design.

### Desired Business Outcome

Reduce avoidable abandonment caused by confusion, uncertainty or unnecessary interaction effort.

---

## 5. Business Objective 03 — Increase Price Transparency

Pricing transparency is important for both user trust and long-term product credibility.

SkyPath should make the relationship between:

**Base Fare + Taxes + Seats + Baggage + Optional Services = Final Price**

easier to understand.

### Desired Business Outcome

Users should reach payment with a clear understanding of the final amount rather than discovering important costs late in the journey.

---

## 6. Business Objective 04 — Support Appropriate Ancillary Selection

Optional services such as baggage, seats, meals or other travel additions can contribute commercial value to an airline booking experience.

However, these services should not be presented in a way that compromises user control.

SkyPath therefore explores a model in which optional services are:

- Clearly identified
- Separated from essential booking decisions
- Accompanied by understandable pricing
- Easy to add
- Easy to decline
- Visible during final review

### Desired Business Outcome

Support relevant ancillary purchases while maintaining user trust and decision transparency.

---

## 7. Business Objective 05 — Reduce Avoidable Support Demand

Confusing interfaces can create unnecessary customer-support interactions.

Users may seek assistance when they cannot determine:

- What they purchased
- Why the price changed
- Whether their payment succeeded
- Where their booking information is located
- What their fare includes
- How to access post-booking information

### Desired Business Outcome

Clearer information architecture and system feedback should reduce avoidable uncertainty and improve self-service.

---

## 8. Business Objective 06 — Strengthen Customer Trust

Trust is particularly important in transactional products where users provide personal information and make financial commitments.

SkyPath aims to establish trust through:

- Transparent pricing
- Predictable interactions
- Clear system feedback
- Consistent visual language
- Understandable booking states
- Accessible information
- Clear confirmation

### Desired Business Outcome

Create an experience that users perceive as reliable, transparent and professionally designed.

---

## 9. Business Objective 07 — Extend the Customer Relationship Beyond Booking

The customer relationship should not end immediately after payment.

Post-booking functionality can provide continued value through:

- Itinerary access
- Booking management
- Boarding information
- Flight status
- Travel updates
- Relevant support

### Desired Business Outcome

Create a continuous travel-management relationship rather than treating booking as an isolated transaction.

---

## 10. Business Objective 08 — Establish a Scalable Product Foundation

The SkyPath experience should be designed as a system rather than as a collection of individually styled screens.

A scalable foundation should support:

- Reusable components
- Consistent interaction patterns
- Clear information architecture
- Extensible navigation
- Accessible design patterns
- Future feature development

### Desired Business Outcome

Create a product foundation that can evolve without requiring fundamental redesign of the entire experience.

---

## 11. Business Goal Prioritisation

| Business Goal | Priority | Expected Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Improve booking completion | Critical | Supports the core commercial transaction |
| Reduce avoidable abandonment | Critical | Removes friction from the booking journey |
| Improve price transparency | Critical | Supports trust and informed purchasing |
| Strengthen customer trust | Critical | Supports long-term product credibility |
| Support relevant ancillary selection | High | Creates opportunities for additional value |
| Reduce avoidable support demand | High | Improves self-service efficiency |
| Extend post-booking engagement | High | Supports continued customer interaction |
| Establish scalable design foundations | High | Enables future product development |

---

## 12. User–Business Alignment

The SkyPath strategy is based on the principle that user and business objectives should reinforce one another.

| User Need | Product Response | Business Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Compare flights easily | Structured comparison | Better decision-making and reduced friction |
| Understand total cost | Transparent pricing | Increased trust |
| Avoid repetitive entry | Efficient passenger flow | Lower interaction effort |
| Understand optional services | Clear add-on structure | More informed ancillary selection |
| Know booking status | Visible system feedback | Reduced uncertainty |
| Review before payment | Consolidated review | Fewer avoidable errors |
| Access booking afterwards | Post-booking management | Continued customer engagement |

The objective is not to optimise one side at the expense of the other.

Instead, SkyPath explores how a better user experience can contribute to stronger product outcomes.

---

## 13. Strategic Design Principle

A central strategic principle is:

> **Reduce friction without reducing transparency.**

Reducing the number of steps is not inherently better if it makes decisions less understandable.

Similarly, exposing every possible option simultaneously can technically increase transparency while making the interface harder to use.

SkyPath therefore focuses on **structured transparency**:

- Show important information at the right decision point.
- Group related information together.
- Separate essential decisions from optional decisions.
- Provide progressive detail where appropriate.
- Maintain access to important information before commitment.

---

## 14. Business Success Perspective

From a product perspective, SkyPath should ultimately contribute to:

- Higher-quality booking journeys
- Lower avoidable friction
- Greater pricing transparency
- Stronger customer confidence
- Better self-service
- More meaningful post-booking engagement
- A scalable product experience

These outcomes provide the strategic foundation for the success metrics defined later in the project.

---

## 15. Scope Boundary

The business goals describe the intended product direction rather than claiming measured commercial performance.

SkyPath is a UX/HCI design project and does not represent a live airline product with production analytics.

Therefore, statements regarding conversion, abandonment, revenue or support reduction are treated as **target outcomes and hypotheses**, not measured results.

Actual validation would require a functioning product, representative users and appropriate behavioural or business data.

---

## 16. Summary

SkyPath seeks to align business viability with human-centred design.

The product should support successful transactions while maintaining clarity, transparency and user control.

The central strategic opportunity is to demonstrate that a well-structured booking experience can simultaneously:

**Reduce friction → Improve understanding → Increase confidence → Support better product outcomes**

This relationship forms the foundation for the product strategy and subsequent design decisions.
