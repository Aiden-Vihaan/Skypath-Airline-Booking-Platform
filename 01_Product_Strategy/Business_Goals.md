# Business Goals

## Primary Goals

- Increase completed flight bookings.
- Improve customer satisfaction and trust.
- Reduce booking abandonment.
- Encourage repeat bookings.
- Increase loyalty programme adoption.

## Secondary Goals

- Improve ancillary revenue through transparent upgrades.
- Strengthen brand reputation.
- Reduce customer support requests through better UX.
- Improve accessibility compliance.
- Build a scalable design system for future product growth.
