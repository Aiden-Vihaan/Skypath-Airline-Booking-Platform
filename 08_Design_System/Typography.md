# SkyPath Typography

## Typography Principles

SkyPath typography is designed to support fast scanning and confident decision-making during flight booking.

The hierarchy distinguishes:

1. Page-level information
2. Flight information
3. Pricing
4. Supporting metadata
5. System feedback

---

## Font Family

Primary font:

**Inter**

Fallback:

**Arial, sans-serif**

Inter is used because of its strong readability across interfaces, compact forms and dense information layouts.

---

## Type Scale

| Style | Size | Weight | Usage |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Display | 40px | 700 | Major landing/page headings |
| H1 | 32px | 700 | Primary page headings |
| H2 | 24px | 700 | Section headings |
| H3 | 18px | 600 | Component headings |
| Body Large | 18px | 400 | Important supporting text |
| Body | 16px | 400 | Default content |
| Body Small | 14px | 400 | Secondary information |
| Caption | 12px | 500 | Metadata and labels |

---

## Flight Information

Flight times and prices should receive stronger visual emphasis than secondary airline information.

Example hierarchy:

**08:30 → 11:45**

London → Dubai

Non-stop · 7h 15m

**€420**

This allows users to scan the most important decision-making information first.

---

## Typography Rules

- Avoid excessive use of uppercase text.
- Use bold weight to establish hierarchy rather than excessive font size.
- Maintain consistent line height.
- Never use typography alone to communicate error or success.
- Prices should remain visually prominent throughout the booking journey.
