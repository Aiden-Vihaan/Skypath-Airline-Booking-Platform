
# SkyPath Motion Guidelines

## Purpose

Motion should provide feedback, continuity and orientation without distracting users during a high-stakes booking process.

---

## Motion Principles

### 1. Functional

Animation should communicate what changed.

### 2. Predictable

Transitions should follow consistent timing.

### 3. Fast

Booking interactions should feel responsive.

### 4. Accessible

Users who prefer reduced motion should receive simplified transitions.

---

## Recommended Timing

| Interaction | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Button feedback | 100–150ms |
| Small component transition | 150–200ms |
| Panel transition | 200–300ms |
| Modal transition | 200–300ms |
| Page transition | 250–350ms |

---

## Examples

### Flight Selection

Selecting a flight should provide immediate visual confirmation.

### Seat Selection

The selected seat should transition between available and selected states without excessive animation.

### Payment

Payment processing should display clear progress feedback and prevent accidental duplicate submissions.

---

## Reduced Motion

When reduced motion is enabled:

- Remove non-essential animations.
- Avoid large movement across the screen.
- Use opacity or instant state changes where appropriate.
