# SkyPath Accessibility Guidelines

## Objective

SkyPath should support travellers with different physical, visual, cognitive and technological needs.

Accessibility is considered throughout the booking journey rather than treated as a final validation step.

---

## Perceivable

### Colour

Do not rely on colour alone to communicate:

- Flight availability
- Errors
- Booking status
- Seat selection

Use labels, icons and supporting text.

### Text

Text must remain readable across device sizes and support browser zoom.

### Images and Icons

Meaningful images require alternative text.

Decorative imagery should not interrupt assistive technology.

---

## Operable

Interactive controls should have sufficiently large touch targets.

Target:

**Minimum 44 × 44px**

Keyboard users should be able to:

- Navigate controls
- Complete forms
- Select flights
- Choose seats
- Review booking
- Complete payment

Focus states must remain clearly visible.

---

## Understandable

Forms should:

- Use clear labels
- Provide examples where necessary
- Explain errors near the relevant field
- Preserve entered information after validation errors

Complex airline terminology should be explained through contextual help.

---

## Error Prevention

High-risk actions should provide confirmation.

Examples:

- Cancel booking
- Remove passenger
- Change flight
- Purchase additional baggage
- Complete payment

---

## Cognitive Accessibility

Reduce unnecessary cognitive load by:

- Showing information progressively
- Grouping related information
- Maintaining consistent terminology
- Providing clear progress indicators
- Showing prices before confirmation
- Avoiding unexpected changes

---

## Inclusive Traveller Support

The interface should support scenarios involving:

- Senior travellers
- Travellers with disabilities
- Families travelling with children
- Users with limited digital confidence
- Users accessing the service on mobile devices

---

## Accessibility Goal

Accessibility should be integrated into the design system so that accessible behaviour becomes the default behaviour of SkyPath components.
