# SkyPath — Research Objectives

## 1. Primary Objective

The primary objective of the SkyPath user research is to understand how travellers search, compare, evaluate and book flights, with particular attention to the moments where uncertainty, cognitive load or unnecessary interaction can affect the booking experience.

The research will translate these observations into actionable UX requirements for the SkyPath platform.

---

## 2. Secondary Objectives

### 2.1 Understand Flight Search Behaviour

Identify how travellers begin a flight search and which information they consider essential at the beginning of the journey.

Areas of interest include:

- Origin and destination
- Travel dates
- Passenger count
- Trip type
- Flexibility
- Preferred departure time

---

### 2.2 Understand Comparison Behaviour

Determine which attributes users compare when evaluating multiple flight options.

Key attributes include:

- Price
- Duration
- Number of stops
- Departure time
- Arrival time
- Airline
- Baggage allowance
- Fare conditions

The objective is to understand not only what information users look at, but how they use it to make a decision.

---

### 2.3 Identify Sources of Decision Uncertainty

Investigate situations where users hesitate, revisit previous screens or require additional information before selecting a flight.

Potential sources include:

- Unclear pricing
- Hidden additional costs
- Complicated fare rules
- Unclear baggage allowances
- Long or inconvenient itineraries
- Uncertainty about cancellation or modification conditions

---

### 2.4 Understand Fare Selection

Explore how travellers interpret different fare categories and determine whether the additional cost of a higher fare provides meaningful value.

The research should identify whether users understand:

- What is included
- What is excluded
- What can be changed
- What can be cancelled
- What baggage is included
- Which benefits justify a higher price

---

### 2.5 Identify Booking Friction

Identify interactions that may unnecessarily increase the effort required to complete a booking.

Particular attention should be given to:

- Passenger information entry
- Repetitive form fields
- Seat selection
- Baggage selection
- Optional services
- Error correction
- Payment preparation

---

### 2.6 Understand Price Perception

Explore how travellers interpret the relationship between the advertised fare and the final amount payable.

The research should investigate whether users expect clear visibility of:

- Base fare
- Taxes
- Baggage charges
- Seat charges
- Optional services
- Final total

---

### 2.7 Understand Information Priorities

Determine which information users need immediately and which information can be revealed progressively.

This objective will help establish an appropriate information hierarchy for SkyPath.

---

### 2.8 Understand Confidence Before Payment

Identify what users need to see or verify before committing to a booking.

Relevant factors may include:

- Selected flight
- Fare type
- Passenger details
- Baggage
- Seat
- Total price
- Cancellation conditions
- Payment information

---

### 2.9 Understand Post-Booking Expectations

Explore what travellers expect after successfully completing a booking.

Potential requirements include:

- Booking confirmation
- Itinerary access
- Booking reference
- Flight status
- Boarding information
- Booking management
- Cancellation or modification
- Travel reminders

---

## 3. Behavioural Objectives

The research should identify observable behaviours rather than relying only on stated preferences.

The study should examine:

- How users scan search results
- What information they notice first
- How they compare alternatives
- When they open additional information
- When they return to previous screens
- Where they hesitate
- What causes reassurance
- What causes abandonment or reconsideration

---

## 4. Cognitive Objectives

The research should investigate the cognitive effort involved in flight booking.

Key areas include:

### Information Load

How much information can users process comfortably when comparing flights?

### Decision Load

How many simultaneous decisions are users expected to make?

### Uncertainty

Which information gaps prevent confident decision-making?

### Memory Load

Which information do users need to remember between different stages?

### Error Risk

Which interactions are most likely to result in incorrect selections?

---

## 5. Accessibility Objectives

The research should also consider whether booking interactions can remain understandable and usable for people with different abilities and levels of digital familiarity.

Areas for investigation include:

- Text clarity
- Visual hierarchy
- Colour dependence
- Form comprehension
- Error messaging
- Keyboard accessibility
- Touch-target size
- Consistent navigation

Accessibility should be treated as part of the core experience rather than a final-stage enhancement.

---

## 6. Product Objectives Derived From Research

The research should help determine whether SkyPath should prioritise:

1. Clear flight comparison
2. Transparent total pricing
3. Understandable fare differences
4. Reduced repetitive data entry
5. Progressive disclosure of complex information
6. Clear separation between required and optional decisions
7. Persistent booking context
8. Strong booking review and confirmation
9. Accessible interaction patterns
10. Useful post-booking management

---

## 7. Research Success Criteria

The research phase will be considered successful when it provides sufficient evidence to:

- Identify recurring traveller behaviours
- Identify meaningful pain points
- Identify important user needs
- Establish distinct user patterns
- Define actionable design opportunities
- Prioritise UX problems
- Support persona development
- Inform information architecture
- Inform user and task flows
- Generate testable design hypotheses

---

## 8. Key Research Question

The objectives ultimately support one central question:

> **How can SkyPath reduce the cognitive and interactional effort required for travellers to compare flight options and confidently complete a booking?**

---

## 9. Expected Design Impact

The findings from these objectives should directly influence the SkyPath experience.

Research findings will be translated into:

**User needs**

→

**UX requirements**

→

**Design principles**

→

**Information architecture**

→

**Interaction patterns**

→

**Interface decisions**

→

**Validation criteria**

This creates a traceable relationship between user research and the final product design.
