# SkyPath — User Research Screener Survey

## 1. Purpose

The screener survey is designed to identify participants who have relevant experience with digital flight-search and booking platforms.

The objective is to ensure that subsequent research activities focus on people who can provide meaningful insight into real or recent travel-booking behaviour.

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

Participants should ideally:

- Be 18 years or older
- Have searched for a flight online
- Have used a website or mobile application to compare flights
- Have completed or seriously considered completing an online flight booking
- Be comfortable describing their booking process and decision-making behaviour

Participants do not need to be frequent travellers.

Occasional travellers are also valuable because their experiences may reveal usability problems that experienced travellers have learned to work around.

# 5. Optional Open-Ended Question

### Describe one thing you find frustrating about booking a flight online.

This question is intentionally open-ended.

It allows participants to identify problems that may not have been anticipated by the research team.

---

# 6. Participant Selection Principles

Participant selection should aim for variation rather than recruiting only one type of traveller.

Where practical, the research sample should include a mixture of:

- Occasional and frequent travellers
- Budget-conscious and convenience-oriented travellers
- Students and working professionals
- Domestic and international travellers
- Different levels of digital familiarity

---

# 7. Exclusion Criteria

Participants may be excluded from the primary research sample when:

- They have never searched for flights online
- They have no experience with digital travel platforms
- They are unable to describe a recent or meaningful booking experience
- Their participation creates a conflict of interest with the project
- Their responses are incomplete or unreliable

---

# 8. Research Ethics

Participation should be voluntary.

Participants should be informed about:

- The purpose of the research
- How their responses will be used
- Whether sessions are recorded
- How their information will be anonymised

No unnecessary personally identifiable information should be stored in the public GitHub repository.

---

# 9. Screening Outcome

The screener should produce a participant pool that represents a useful range of flight-booking experiences.

The purpose is not statistical representation of the entire travelling population.

Instead, the goal is to obtain sufficiently diverse qualitative perspectives to identify recurring behaviours, needs and usability problems.

---

# 10. Relationship to the Research Plan

The screener supports the broader research process by ensuring that interview participants have relevant experience with the problem space.

The resulting participant profiles will inform the interview phase and subsequent synthesis activities.
